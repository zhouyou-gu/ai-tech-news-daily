Ada-TokenCom adapts compression and channel coding together

TokenCom · High Primary preprint August 28, 2026

Timeliness: Still-timely submission in the newest primary communications feed. Trend / relevance: Directly combines token prediction, arithmetic coding, source rate, and modulation-and-coding control under changing channels.

Ada-TokenCom sends informative prefix tokens and lets a matched autoregressive model reconstruct or generate the remainder. A Lyapunov controller jointly selects compression rate and radio coding, and the authors report gains over digital and deep joint source-channel baselines.

Why it matters to the group: It treats tokens as both model state and network payload, exactly the representation-to-transport bridge needed for AI-native communication.

Follow-up: Reproduce one reported rate point, then add burst losses and model mismatch to measure semantic drift when transmitter and receiver priors diverge.

Token-oriented SemCom selects aligned latents without end-to-end retraining

TokenCom + SemCom · High Primary preprint August 26, 2026

Timeliness: Still-timely preprint. Trend / relevance: Directly joins token relevance, learned image compression, client/server transformers, and rate–accuracy tradeoffs.

The approach aligns compressed latents from a learned image codec with vision-transformer tokens and sends only those most relevant to the downstream task. It preserves pretrained client and server models, avoiding a bespoke end-to-end communications training loop.

Why it matters to the group: It is a precise bridge from semantic representation choice to a practical wireless transport budget.

Follow-up: Replicate the rate–accuracy curve, then add fading, retransmission, and unequal error protection to test whether token relevance predicts radio protection value.

Significance-driven SemCom optimizes value per radio resource

SemCom · High Primary preprint August 28, 2026

Timeliness: Still-timely submission in the newest communications feed. Trend / relevance: Introduces a per-sample decision-theoretic value measure, a meta-adaptive transceiver, and semantic-value-aware MAC allocation.

The paper defines semantic spectrum efficiency as task value delivered per unit bandwidth and time. Meta-VIB adapts latents to channel and symbol budgets without online retraining, while a restless-bandit scheduler allocates channels using value of information; the reported pedestrian-monitoring gains reach 1000× at 0 dB.

Why it matters to the group: It supplies a rigorous candidate objective for moving SemCom beyond reconstruction quality toward task utility under shared resources.

Follow-up: Audit the significance estimator under class imbalance, then compare value-aware scheduling with age-of-information and uncertainty-only baselines.

SemCom · High Primary preprint + demo August 27, 2026

Timeliness: Still-timely implementation paper with a live multi-user system. Trend / relevance: Connects SNR, codelength, age of information, task loss, and application response in one observable deployment.

SafeStep extracts pedestrian trajectories and risk labels from four camera feeds, sends them through an AWGN semantic transceiver, and renders user-selectable operating points in browsers. Its compact Meta-VIB model handles varying SNR, codelength, and age without retraining and scales to 100 distinct users with no request failures.

Why it matters to the group: It offers a practical baseline for studying when communication metrics do or do not predict downstream safety behavior.

KV eviction gains a probabilistic formalism and decode-time correction

LLM KV · High Primary preprint August 28, 2026

Timeliness: Still-timely language-model systems submission. Trend / relevance: Formalizes eviction hardness, reframes the retention choice as expectation estimation, and exposes bias in existing zero-variance heuristics.

The paper shows that optimal KV eviction is computationally hard but can be approximated through probabilistic sampling. This view enables correction during decoding for information removed from the cache and improves robustness across tasks at fixed compression budgets.

Why it matters to the group: A principled estimator makes it easier to connect cache compression error to transport, offload, and quality guarantees.

LLM KV · High Primary preprint August 27, 2026

Timeliness: Still-timely long-context inference submission. Trend / relevance: Challenges attention magnitude as a causal-value proxy and adds a training-free repair pass compatible with several existing policies.

TwinKV detects evicted tokens with no surviving near-duplicate and swaps them with retained tokens whose keys are redundant, without changing the original budget or policy score. Results vary by model and baseline, but the paper clearly identifies ceiling cases and task structures where the method does not help.
