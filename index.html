NVIDIA moves agent containment into runtime and hardware

September 28, 2026Fresh signal · current-window launch

Trend / relevance: The open platform has immediate support across infrastructure, security, model and robotics vendors, making runtime enforcement a shared ecosystem layer.

OpenShell places agents inside policy-governed sandboxes, while the Sentry reference design monitors behavior out of band and can quarantine a workload when it crosses a boundary. Filesystem, process, network and credential controls are enforced beneath the agent harness.

Why it matters: Evaluation logs and prompt rules are not enough when agents can alter their environment; independent enforcement creates a stronger basis for long-running and embodied deployments.
